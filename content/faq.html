Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Aurion Audio devices, shipping, and support.

Do your products come with a warranty?

Yes. Every Aurion product ships with a 3-year limited warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship, transferable worldwide.

How do I clean my in-ear monitors?

Use the included dry brush to remove debris from the nozzle and shell. Avoid liquids near the drivers, and store them in the supplied case when not in use.

How long does shipping take?

Orders are dispatched within 1 business day and typically arrive in 2–5 business days with tracking. Express options are available at checkout.

What is your return policy?

You can return any product within 30 days in original condition for a full refund. Contact support first to receive a return authorization.

Which Aurion is right for me?

Choose the A1 for stage and critical listening, the H1 for open-back home listening, or the D1 to drive any headphone from your desk. Tell us how you listen and we will point you to the right model.

Do headphones need "burn-in"?

Not required. Our drivers are measured and matched at the factory; any change in the first few hours is simply the earpads settling. Just listen.

Do you provide firmware updates?

Yes, the D1 and D2 DAC/amplifiers receive free firmware updates over USB. Register your product to be notified.

Which inputs does the D1 support?

The D1 supports USB-C, coaxial, and optical digital inputs, with balanced XLR and single-ended 6.35 mm headphone outputs.

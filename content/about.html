About Aurion Audio

We build professional audio devices for people who can hear the difference — engineers, musicians, and listeners who want the truth, not a flavour.

Our Story

Aurion Audio was founded by a small team of acoustics engineers who were tired of reference gear that measured well in the lab but was exhausting to listen to. We set out to build devices that are neutral on the measurement rig and effortless in real listening.

Everything we make is designed, assembled, and measured in-house. Drivers are hand-matched, enclosures are CNC-milled, and every unit leaves the bench only after it passes a final frequency-response and distortion sweep against its reference target.

What We Believe

  • Neutrality first. We tune to the source, not to the room.
  • Measure, then listen. Data and ears have to agree.
  • Built to last. Repairable designs and a 3-year warranty.
  • Honest support. Talk to the people who built your gear.
